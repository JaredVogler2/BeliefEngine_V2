# Phase 12 — Premium original visual and audio polish that reinforces mechanics

Transform the verified playable simulation into an expressive, coherent product. Prioritize **readable cause-and-effect** ahead of decoration. No generic template-dashboard aesthetics.

## Art direction

Working aesthetic: **hand-illustrated storybook strategy diorama** with cozy unsettling village, illuminated manuscripts and occult natural philosophy. Dark evergreen night shading; parchment/gold UI; layered forests, luminous river, brick/clay roofs, distinct household motifs; ink trails for rumors and magical alterations. Avoid copyrighted or ripped game assets. Favor original hand-coded procedural assets, SVG/vector icons, generated sprites only when necessary and properly licensed. Set a restrained palette with AA contrast for UI copy. Capture an asset license/source ledger.

Use a 3/4 orthographic view (or isometric if hit testing and panning already robust):
- River animated as changing physically bounded water contours; flood edge visually progresses based on tile-state.
- Buildings have recognizably different silhouettes: house, mill, farm, hall, bridge, bell tower, beacons, refuge.
- Citizens small but legible with role-based outfits/icons and name/hover/focus identity.
- Terrain visual variation via authored tile shapes and local noise, not a repeated checkerboard.
- Night/day transitions are atmospherically meaningful, but gameplay only advances on End Day.
- Villagers continue subtle idle motion during planning without changing authoritative state.

## Feedback that shows true causality

Represent event types with distinct signatures:
- Claim introduced: warm ink spiral from speaker.
- Trusted gossip: dotted gold trajectory along an ACTUAL graph edge; hostile denial dotted cool/rough stroke.
- Evidence: localized staged effect with witness icons; witnesses highlight.
- Belief threshold: expanding glyph around affected landmark + ground overlay.
- Newly activated law: world bends with a brief scale/distortion or shader effect; physical tiles animate toward NEW ENGINE STATE.
- Side effect: contrasting warning effect (burned fields, displaced rain, coughing villagers).
- Counterpropaganda: contested rings pulse, followed by measurable confidence decrease.
- Victory/loss: brief scene-specific treatment, always skippable.

The animation must faithfully follow event log deltas; do not spawn celebratory effects for laws that didn't truly activate. Use object pooling and effect count caps to keep low-end mobiles smooth.

## Audio

No mandatory downloaded proprietary music. Build a small adaptive original score with lightweight loops or Web Audio synthesis; support silent mode:
- Ambient wind, soft creek, wood creaks; intensity ramps near disaster.
- Distinct short SFX for seed rumor, evidence, contact, law activation, warning, day advance, successful rescue, defeat.
- Bell sound spatially/geographically connected to bell tower action.
- UI clicks/hover subtle, not constantly annoying.
- Music and SFX separate settings, remembered locally; browser audio unlock after gesture.
- Mute button visible, honor reduced motion and if practical reduced data/preferences.
- Avoid autoplay violations, infinite oscillator leaks and overlapping scene audio.

## UI polish passes

- Stable typography system: headings, HUD numerals, descriptive text, tooltips, narrative.
- Meaningful microinteraction feedback under 150ms perceived delay for input.
- Animations mostly 120–300ms; large major-law activation may use ~1s, skippable.
- Icons illustrated and matched to law motifs. Distinguish law effective radius and affected buildings.
- Modal transitions and focus trapping correct, no hidden dialogs trapping keys.
- Meaningful empty states (no valid action, law countered, isolated village).
- Character cards concise and emotional, not walls of autogenerated prose.
- Clear accessibility labels, keyboard focus outlines, scalable text.

## Small-screen art rules

At 320px width, keep core threat/day/AP and contextual primary CTA readable. Avoid side drawers that obscure entire scene. Show map with zoom/pan and focus-to-target affordance. On desktop, use two-column map+panel with resizable or foldable overlays as needed. Avoid tiny hover-only village entities: clicking an enlarged hit region still selects intended citizen.

## Quality validation

Check screenshots on 320px, 390px, tablet and 1440px; compare multiple game states (calm, law active, hazard, debrief). Test no missing textures, text clipping, offscreen tooltips, uncontrolled camera drifts, inaccessible modals. Check frame stability under simultaneous rumor and flooding effects. A muted/reduced-motion user must still get all mechanically relevant information.

## Acceptance

A complete original aesthetic with coherent HUD, key SFX and non-blocking ambience, and transformations visually tied to actual state. Preserve all regression test traces. Provide actual screenshots/evidence if tools permit. Commit only sourced/licensed assets or generated source code; no huge unnecessary files.

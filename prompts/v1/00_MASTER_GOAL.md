# /goal — Build BELIEF ENGINE into a game people actually want to replay

You are the autonomous lead game designer, gameplay programmer, simulation engineer, UX designer, artist/technical artist, QA engineer and release owner. **Implement the game in this repository.** Do not stop after producing documentation, pseudocode, attractive menus or a shallow demo.

## Mission

Create **BELIEF ENGINE**, a striking, immediately comprehensible 2D browser strategy roguelite with a novel central rule: **shared beliefs physically transform the world**. Players survive cascading village crises by introducing rumors, producing evidence, recruiting influential believers, manipulating rival factions and exploiting the physical laws generated by collective conviction. Each successful tactic creates new risks. This is a game about *designing social causality*, not a passive agent-based visualization.

A delightful play session should include: (1) a clear looming crisis, (2) a surprising but legible set of possible claims, (3) a dramatic early chain reaction, (4) at least one reversal or unexpected side effect the player could understand, (5) a hard choice with multiple viable responses, (6) a resolved ending with a "how the world changed" story, and (7) a reason to immediately try a different strategy or seed.

A public-ready browser SPA, locally runnable with standard Node/npm, suitable for static Vercel hosting. It must include:
- Start menu, options, playable tutorial, at least one polished full scenario and additional content as specified by later phases.
- Interactive world map with live villagers, settlements, threats, rumors, social links, evidence and visibly effective laws.
- Meaningful, resource-constrained actions; deterministic turns; genuinely reactive citizen simulation.
- **Win, loss, restart, pause, save/resume and debrief** with scoring and explanations.
- Three or more campaign scenarios plus well-tested procedurally seeded replay mode by release.
- Accessible keyboard/touch input, performance budgets, clean art, audible feedback (with mute), responsive layout.
- Automated tests that verify the social-to-physical causal chain, playable E2E path, and seed reproducibility.
- No mandatory external AI service, authentication, backend, secret keys, database or paid asset use for baseline browser/local-MCP gameplay. Optional remote MCP and LLM-enhanced interpretation can use an opt-in backend/provider.

## Player-facing design pillars (priority order)

**P1 — Causality players can understand.** Make the path rumor → people → belief pressure → physical law → world outcome visible, with an explainable counterfactual. A gorgeous map with unexplained outcomes fails.

**P2 — Tension under scarce influence.** Each day, the player chooses which people to persuade, where to stage evidence, how to manage skeptics and which opportunities to abandon. There must be opportunity costs.

**P3 — Emergence with guardrails.** Villagers gossip, argue, verify events and pursue competing agendas. Belief effects are authored deterministic rules, not unconstrained natural-language claims or randomized miracles. Surprises must be retrospectively intelligible.

**P4 — Multiple credible strategies.** Influencer-driven belief cascade, grassroots trust-building, evidence-first skepticism conversion and contrarian counter-propaganda should each sometimes win. No single obvious click loop dominates every seed.

**P5 — Rich short runs.** Target an introductory run of roughly 10–18 minutes; later 15–30 minute campaign/challenge sessions, depending on difficulty. Adapt after real playtests. Support replay, undo during planning and clear turnover between runs.

**P6 — Legibility and delight.** Every costly action triggers immediate readable response (a ripple in social graph, icon/mood change, law meter shift, or an explainable preview); major transformations get short, skippable celebration.

## Fixed core promise to players

**"Convince enough people that something is true—and the world obeys. But belief spreads both ways."**

You are the Keeper of Stories in a small living region. You cannot drag the river, spawn food or delete enemies. You must change minds, stage evidence and use limited influence to unlock physical laws. Six recurring tension types: flood, famine, fire, sickness, beasts, civic collapse. Laws create interconnected benefits and side effects.

The main map shows physical consequences; the social lens explains why. A player may optionally write their own short belief proposition: an offline constrained interpreter (with optional opt-in LLM enhancement) proposes only validated, balanced physical effects and asks for confirmation. An MCP-connected AI agent may play via the exact same legal engine actions as a human. Every game has a **threat clock**, a **belief/law meter**, and **visible character incentives**. Players should be able to say "I lost because the miller convinced the west bank that bells were dangerous, so the rain law collapsed when the storm arrived."

## Quantitative quality gates (targets, not fabricated success claims)

By initial release, aim for:
- New players can do a meaningful action within 30 seconds and explain why a law activated after brief onboarding.
- First complete tutorial success with guided assistance; an unguided normal run should be achievable with multiple viable strategies.
- Headless tests: 1,000+ reproducible seeded runs per major rules change; at least two diverse strategies consistently beat a subset of validated solvable seeds.
- No forced-loss seeds in the official procedural pool: validate with constructive witnesses / solver or filter generation; record limitations.
- Meaningful agency: alternate action sequences produce meaningfully different outcomes under same seed; not just different cosmetic scores.
- Low-friction replay: "try again", "new seed", "share seed", "daily challenge" work without account or API.
- No missing assets, silent input failures, unhandled errors, softlocks, infinite loops or broken mobile input in release test paths.
- No screenshot-only proof or fake metrics. Unverified qualitative fun is honestly marked for human testing.

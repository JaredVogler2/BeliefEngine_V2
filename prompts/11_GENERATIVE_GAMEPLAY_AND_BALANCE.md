# Phase 11 — Keep an exciting strategy game rather than an AI wish machine

## Modes

Classic uses V1 content, works without model or internet, retains balance and local scores. Generative Chronicle offers novel claims and new social reactions with separate leaderboards/records. Living Village emphasizes memory-based discussion and evidence conflicts while preserving win/loss. Reality Lab is explicitly unranked for exploring more powerful but still bounded laws.

## Tradeoffs

New law has a real writing/introducing influence cost, social proof threshold, independent witness requirement, physical catalyst, duration/upkeep, observable downside, and conflict interaction. Its strength depends on effect magnitude/scope, not words the player uses or the model's enthusiasm. Strong lasting magic requires higher adoption, more evidence or more material costs. Do not permit "infinite free food" or "everyone instantly believes me" in balanced modes; even unranked experiment mode must preserve CPU/memory safety.

## Strategic events

Implement genuinely emergent, grounded events:
- skeptical agent asks for a reproducible test, then converts after directly seeing evidence;
- a town leader opposes a powerful law because it damages public livelihood;
- player chooses between winning quickly with hazardous magic and preserving social trust;
- a two-clause claim creates unintended side effect, prompting opposition;
- an NPC invents a limited competing claim using same cost/capability validator;
- social network geography isolates one population, requiring bridge repair or messenger;
- conflicting laws require player to negotiate which coalition to cultivate.

Dialogue is brief and based on actual events. Rule activations require genuine social success, not a pop-up after successful LLM call.

## Progression and replayability

Daily/expedition seeds as V1, plus varied permitted primitives, social starting relationships and weather/physics problems. Player learns laws by experimentation, not by waiting for model generation. A failure gives counterfactual explanation and one-click retry same seed or new seed. Optional Atlas records invented laws/memorable conversations but avoids infinitely dominant permanent power boosts. No compulsive streaks or purchasable advantage.

## Balance tests

Headless same-seed policies: no-op, spam a broad law, evidence-first, bridge-based persuasion, inventive compound effect, careful low-power synergy and rival antagonist. All use same legal engine action API. Replay stored LLM proposals for tournaments, no thousands of live calls. Measure AP spent, law activation time, survival, collateral damage, strategies diversity, social network reach, latency/cost and fun dead time. Fix one dominant free win exploit. Human fun subjective; don't fabricate playtests.

## Gate

At least two very different creative laws provide viable strategies, a third can backfire, and one meaningful win/lose arc can be completed within reasonable session length. The model adds strategic variety rather than only more text.

# Phase 09 — Complete causality: imagine → persuade → manifest → consequences

## Critical gate

A successful model call is NOT a successful game. The user writes a creative statement, the model produces a grounded executable interpretation, villagers genuinely spread/dispute it through the social network, and only sufficient public belief plus catalysts activates the physical rule. The result changes survival/world state and the real 3D display. Verify this whole chain.

## Exact integration

1. Composer accepts user text, calls optional live interpreter or explicitly limited offline reader.
2. Validate typed AST, scene entity IDs, condition/event semantics, physical operator capability and power budget. Unsupported/partial clauses are explained, never silently relabeled.
3. Preview target, effect, radius, duration, upkeep, opposition, population threshold, distinct supporters required, physical catalyst and side effects.
4. Player confirms hashed AST bound to current revision. Normal COMPOSE action spends influence; add claim to same social engine as built-in laws. Prevent duplicate charges, stale/invalid confirmation, unsafely broad laws.
5. Player introduces claim to a villager and spends another normal legal action. The engine computes social propagation over trust edges, direct observation and model-based NPC **proposed** speech/actions. Only the engine changes conviction and AP.
6. Resolve local social support/threshold, evidence and catalysts with hysteresis. No free activation because the LLM said "the whole village believes."
7. Compile accepted law into deterministic RulePlan, invoke effect handlers against world state, log property and object deltas.
8. If a competing law or new evidence collapses belief pressure, update law stability/effects; irreversible burned/destroyed structures stay destroyed.
9. Debrief explains provenance: player → witnesses → supporting network → activation threshold → specific world property changed → survival/damage outcome.

## Three proof claims

A. "Chickens fly when the bell rings" → new supported chicken archetype, bounded upward force + movement and real obstacle crossing; if not truly modeled, choose and implement another new object effect instead, never fake motion alone.
B. "Wet stone becomes light beside lanterns" → material density/weight and placement/carrying consequences; reduce support or extinguish lantern and effect ends as defined.
C. "At night, bell ringing pulls rain toward the orchard and makes local flames burn weaker" → compound hydrology + fire effects with downside, actual rain redistributes and fire behavior changes.

At least one meaningful opponent distrusts one claim and causes social counteraction. At least one invented belief creates unintended consequential physical harm and makes player choose a different strategy.

## Conflicts and explanations

Two laws targeting same property with opposed effects resolve by policy based on strength and support, never accidental update order. Localized climate/force effects cannot silently affect global world. Every important physical change has event ID and reasoning for tooltip. Unsupported text offers clarification or model limits rather than imaginary equivalence.

## Tests and gate

Unit checks below-threshold does nothing, above-threshold with catalyst changes canonical state, physical change isn't only 3D particle effect; multiple law conflicts stable. Actual browser full-input composer → belief → law → 3D outcome for three claims plus a complete win and loss. Replay exact hash from stored LLM outputs with network disabled. Original V1 golden tests remain green.
